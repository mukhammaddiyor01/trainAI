How this preview is limited

TrainAI accepts an explicit 1–250 kcal/day change for gain or loss, or 0 for maintenance. The change must also stay within 10% of your current estimated maintenance; the result must be at least 1,500 kcal/day. Current and target BMI must fall from 18.5 to below 40. If your current weight reaches or crosses your saved target, review the goal first.

These experimental application limits are not medical recommendations or proof that a calorie amount meets your needs. The calculation applies only to eligible adults using the DRI 2023 maintenance estimate. It does not predict a completion date, select foods, or approve a plan.
