How do the activity categories compare?

The source uses physical activity level (PAL), a whole-day measure. Inactive includes daily living activities; the higher categories include progressively more activity. These are reference categories, not exercise recommendations.

  • Inactive: PAL 1.00 to below 1.53
  • Low active: PAL 1.53 to below 1.68
  • Active: PAL 1.68 to below 1.85
  • Very active: PAL 1.85 to below 2.50
